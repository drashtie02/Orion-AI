Iron Man - Tony Stark

Tony Stark is a genius inventor and billionaire industrialist who created the Iron Man armor. After being captured by terrorists and forced to build weapons, Stark instead constructed a powered suit of armor to escape. This experience transformed him from a weapons manufacturer into a superhero dedicated to protecting the world.

The Iron Man suit is equipped with advanced technology including repulsor rays, flight capabilities, and an AI assistant named JARVIS (later FRIDAY). The suit's arc reactor powers all its systems and also keeps shrapnel from reaching Stark's heart.

As a founding member of the Avengers, Iron Man has fought alongside heroes like Captain America, Thor, Hulk, Black Widow, and Hawkeye. Despite having no superpowers, his intelligence and technology make him one of Earth's mightiest defenders.

Tony Stark's character arc involves struggling with ego, alcoholism, and the responsibility of his inventions. He evolves from a self-centered playboy to a selfless hero willing to sacrifice everything for others.

Key abilities: Genius-level intellect, master engineer, powered armor suit with weapons systems, flight, superhuman strength (when wearing armor), energy projection through repulsor technology.

Notable enemies include Iron Monger, Whiplash, Mandarin, and Ultron (an AI he accidentally created that became evil).

Iron Man's base of operations is Stark Tower (later Avengers Tower) in New York City, and he runs Stark Industries, a technology and defense company.
